Kya Banaye?

Kya Banaye is a simple meal-decision app for Indian households.

Instead of searching through hundreds of recipes, the app helps you decide what to prepare for breakfast, lunch and dinner.

It considers your preferences, cooking time, available ingredients and recent meals to give practical suggestions.

Important

Kya Banaye provides suggestions only. It does not provide medical, dietary or nutritional advice. Users should use their own judgement and consult an appropriate professional where necessary.
